# Assumptions and open questions

## Assumptions for Prompt 1

- The public canonical origin is provisionally `https://orindle.com`; deployment must override it if the final hostname differs.
- “Orindle” wordmark and a simple original geometric mark may be created because no existing brand assets are present.
- Public author identity can be “Orindle Editorial” without inventing a named person or credentials.
- Content dates use the actual initial publication date, 2026-11-05, and are updated only on substantive revisions.
- Legal pages are transparent product-policy drafts and require qualified legal review before commercial launch.
- Contact details are not invented. Until a verified support inbox is configured, the contact experience offers an explicit local preview or routes to a real configured channel only.

## Open questions / launch blockers

1. What is the final production domain and verified support email?
2. Which legal entity name, registered address and governing law should appear in Terms/Privacy?
3. Which model IDs and providers are actually enabled through Vercel AI Gateway at launch?
4. What are credit expiry, transfer, tax, regional pricing and statutory cancellation terms after legal/finance review?
5. What is the exact signup/login destination during staged rollout?
6. What retention periods and subprocessors must the production Privacy Policy enumerate?
7. Is customer consent required for cookies/analytics in target markets, and which consented analytics system will be used?
8. What accessibility assistive-technology matrix and real-user performance telemetry will be available before launch?

None of these questions blocks the static frontend. They do block truthful production claims, real payments or launch sign-off.
